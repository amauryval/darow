# Darow — Possible improvements (Vue 3 UI)

> **Role** — undecided leads for `web/`: what would improve the experience without the decision
> being made. Nothing here is planned. A lead taken up leaves this file and becomes an entry of
> [todo.md](todo.md); a lead dropped becomes an entry of
> [limitations.md](limitations.md) or disappears.
> Neighbours: [blockers.md](blockers.md) · [spec.md](spec.md) (rules and
> architecture).
>
> Go server: [../back/improvements.md](../back/improvements.md) · Project: [../project/improvements.md](../project/improvements.md).

Each entry carries a stable identifier (`IMP-Fn`); a retired identifier is never reused.

**The *Worth doing — and when* column** is the audit's own verdict on the lead, and it is not the
decision: it says whether the lead looks worth its cost today, and names the **signal** that would
settle it — a sixth screen needing the same shape, a first paint measured as slow, a menu being
opened for another reason anyway. A trigger is an observable event, never a date and never "when
there is time". A lead whose signal fires stops being a lead: it leaves for [todo.md](todo.md) with
a new `TODO-Fn`, or for [limitations.md](limitations.md) if the signal is what rules it out.

| IMP-F13 | Offer the reload on the table's own page too: `TableDetailView` knows the table's `sourceName`/`sourceLayer` (the metadata carries them now), so *Recharger depuis la source* could open the same form the sources screen opens | A table whose file was updated is found from the table, not from the file — the operator looking at stale rows is on the table's page, and today has to go back to `/sources` and pick the right row. What has to be decided is whether the CRS question is asked again there or taken from the table's stored `srid` | **Yes — the most worthwhile of the three, and the smallest.** The CRS question left open opposite has a defensible default: take the table's stored `srid` and do not ask it twice. Settled that way, the chantier is one *Actions* entry plus the form that already exists. Do it the next time `TableDetailView`'s *Actions* menu is opened for anything else | §6 |
| IMP-F14 | Decide whether the production bundle is split further. `mise run ui-build` emits two chunks past Vite's 500 kB warning: the map's route chunk at ~670 kB (~195 kB gzipped, essentially OpenLayers) and the entry chunk at ~474 kB (~147 kB gzipped). The map is already lazy — only a visit to a table's page pays it — so what is left to decide is the entry chunk: whether the dashboard's chart layer and the OIDC client, neither of which the first paint needs, become chunks of their own | Nothing is broken and no rule is violated, which is why this is a lead and not a chantier: the figures are gzipped sizes a local network hides entirely. It matters for a first visit over a slow link, where §8.10's perceived-performance concern starts at the bundle rather than at the skeletons. What has to be decided is whether that visit is a case this product cares about — an admin UI reached from an office is not a landing page — before adding split points that make the build harder to read for a gain nobody measures | **No, not now** — nobody has measured a cost, and an admin UI reached from an office network pays none. The signal has to be a measurement rather than a number read off the build log: a first paint actually slow on a link someone uses, or the entry chunk growing past what one more split point could still fix | §8.10, §10 |
| IMP-F15 | Extract the read-side counterpart of `useAsyncAction` — the single-shot resource loader — and rebuild the composables that hand-write it on top of it. The shape is `data` + `loading` + `loadedOnce` + `error`, a `load()` that raises `loading`, clears `error`, awaits one call, sets `loadedOnce`, catches into `errorMessage(e, '<French fallback>')` and lowers `loading` in `finally`, and a `retry` aliased to it — written out identically in `useDiagnostics`, `useDashboardStats`, `useTableSettingsOverrides`, `useTableSettings` and `useTableMetadata`, with `useTableProvenance` a partial variant. What differs between them is only the call, the fallback message, and what triggers the first load: `onMounted` for the first three, `watch(tableId, …, { immediate: true })` for the last two, which vue-router's view reuse makes mandatory (§9). A `useResource(fetch, fallbackMessage, { key? })` covering both triggers would leave each of those composables holding its own subject alone — the mutations, the caches, the derived columns | It is the most repeated shape in the application, because §0.18 makes every server-fed area answer for its four states and each composable answers on its own. The duplication is not the line count but the drift surface: `useColumnDistinctValues` sets `error` to `null` where the five above set it to `''`, `useTableProvenance` never raises `loading` at all, and a view reading two of them has to know which convention each follows. What has to be decided is whether one more level of composable is worth it here — `usePaginatedList` already proved the pattern for lists, but a loader is thinner, and a composable whose whole body becomes one `useResource` call may read as indirection rather than as its subject | **Not yet — and the trigger is drift, not the count.** Five hand-written loaders are tolerable while they agree, and the disagreements opposite are still invisible to a user. Do it when a sixth server-fed screen needs the shape, or the first time that disagreement produces a rendering bug — and then convert all of them in one pass, never the newcomer alone, since a half-converted set makes the drift permanent | §0.18, §4 |
